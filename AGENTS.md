# AI Project Contract

Binding for agents in this repository.

## Before editing

- Read this file, README, CONTRIBUTING, `git status`.
- Work on a topic branch. Do not commit to `main`.
- Public docs stay product pages: what it is, how to run it, license.
- Do not list held domains, fleet names, or origin IPs in this repo.

## During editing

- Keep the landing to: hostname, one Chinese sentence, one English sentence, contact email.
- White background. No extra labels, status chips, or decoration.
- Contact email lives in `site/index.html`.

## Before commit

- Conventional Commit: `type(scope): imperative summary`
- `test -f site/index.html` and grep the contact address plus the English sale sentence.
- Push the branch. Open a PR. Do not push `main`.
